# 🎂 Rebecca's Chrono-Quest: A Birthday Time-Travel Adventure

A custom retro 8-bit Choose Your Own Adventure game created for **Rebecca's Birthday** by **Lucas**!

## 🚀 How to Play
No installation or command line needed!
1. Open the folder `/Users/lucas/.cline/data/workspaces/chat/rebecca-birthday-adventure`
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to launch the game in any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

## ✨ Game Highlights Tailored for Rebecca
- **Interactive Retro CRT Arcade GUI**: Toggleable CRT scanlines, dynamic color palettes for each era, animated pixel-art scenes, and a built-in Web Audio 8-bit sound & music synthesizer.
- **The Official Cat-Dex Party**:
  - 🧡 **Jinny** (2021) — Food-motivated kibble & pasta inspector
  - 🤍 **Joonie** (2021) — Velcro cuddle bug with cute little dirty eyes
  - 🌟 **Billie** (2022–2024) — Sweet Star-Guardian watching over every timeline
  - 🚂 **Tommy ("Choo Choo" / Thomas the Tank Engine)** (2024) — High-speed hallway locomotive zoomies
- **Real Memories & Multiverse Timelines**:
  - **2021**: Coastal dunes in **Kitty Hawk** & bringing home baby **Jinny & Joonie**
  - **2022**: Golden sunbeams & starlight tribute with sweet **Billie**
  - **2023**: Aesthetic coffee shops, fusion pasta, and neon nights in **South Korea**
  - **2024**: Adopting zooming **Tommy ("Choo Choo!")**
  - **2026**: Celebrating your **5-Year Anniversary in Cancun** + **Rebecca's Birthday**
  - **Multiverse & Future**: **Castle Pasta-Lot (The Royal Cat Kingdom)**, **The Intergalactic Choo-Choo Express**, and **Year 2076 (50 Years in the Future)**
- **7 Unlockable Endings & Souvenir Inventory**: Collect items across timelines to unlock secret paths and track all 7 endings!
- **Personal Birthday Note**: Click **💌 LUCAS'S NOTE** at the top (or customize it via **⚙ CUSTOMIZE DETAILS** on the title screen).
